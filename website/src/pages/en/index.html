Is Gentle free?

Yes. You can create an account for free and use part of the stories, meditations and sounds. Premium gives you everything.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, Android and any modern browser. With the same account you can pick up where you left off anywhere.

Can I cancel Premium at any time?

Yes. Cancel through the App Store, Google Play or your account page, depending on where you subscribed. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

Does Gentle help with insomnia?

Many people fall asleep more easily with calm audio. Gentle is not a treatment, though. If you've been sleeping badly for a while, talk to your doctor.
