Questions about paying

Can't find your question? Visit the help page.

How do I pay?

On iPhone you pay through the App Store, on Android through Google Play. In the browser you pay securely by card, iDEAL or other methods through Stripe.

How do I cancel?

Cancel where you subscribed: in your App Store or Google Play settings, or through Manage subscription in the web app. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Does Premium work on all my devices?

Yes. Premium belongs to your account. Log in on another device with the same account and everything is ready.

I've paid but don't see Premium

Go to your profile in the app and choose Restore purchases. If that doesn't work, get in touch through the help page.
