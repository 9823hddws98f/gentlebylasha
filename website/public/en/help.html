Help and FAQ

Stuck? Email hallo@gentle.example. We usually reply within two working days.

Account and signing in

I forgot my password

Choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen. You'll get an email with a link to set a new password. Check your spam folder too.

Can I sign in with Apple or Google?

Yes. Use the same sign-in method on every device and you'll see the same account everywhere.

How do I change the language?

Gentle follows your device language. You can also change it yourself in your profile.

Listening

How do I listen offline?

Tap the download icon on a story, meditation or sound. You'll find everything under Downloads in your profile.

The sound stops when my screen turns off

Check that battery saving or background restrictions are off for Gentle. On Android you'll find this in the app settings under Battery.

How do I set a reminder?

Go to your profile and choose Reminders. Pick a time and allow notifications when your device asks.

Premium and payments

How do I cancel Premium?

Through the App Store or Google Play if you subscribed there. If you paid in the browser, choose Manage subscription in the web app.

Restore purchases

New device? Sign in with the same account and choose Restore purchases in your profile.

Delete your account

You can delete your account and all related data yourself, in the app or in the browser.

  1. Open Gentle and sign in.
  2. Go to your profile.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

We then delete your profile, favourites, download list and listening history. A running subscription through the App Store or Google Play has to be cancelled there separately.

Can't sign in anymore? Email hallo@gentle.example from your account's email address and we'll take care of it.
