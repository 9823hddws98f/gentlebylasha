Small habits, calmer nights

Gentle works best alongside a few simple habits.

Around the same time every day

Your body loves rhythm. Going to bed and getting up at set times, weekends included, makes falling asleep easier.

Set a bedtime in Gentle and you'll get a calm reminder to start winding down.

Screen away, ears open

Start your story, put your phone face down and close your eyes.

Cool and dark

A cool, dark bedroom helps. Gentle takes care of the sound.

Caffeine early in the day

Coffee and tea with caffeine linger for hours. Keep them to the morning.

Write it down
