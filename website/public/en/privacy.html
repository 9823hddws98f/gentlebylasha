Privacy

In short: we only process what's needed to make Gentle work, and we don't sell anything on.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Who we are

Gentle is responsible for processing your personal data as described in this statement. You can reach us at hallo@gentle.example.

What data we process

  • Account details: your name, email address and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the details they share with us.
  • Usage: what you listen to, your favourites, downloads and your check-in answers.
  • Subscription: whether you have Premium, through which platform and until when. Payment details themselves are handled by the App Store, Google Play or Stripe, not by us.
  • Technical: a device token to send reminders, if you turn them on.

Why

We use this data to run your account, show your progress and favourites on all your devices, check your subscription, send reminders you set yourself and improve the app.

Who we share it with

Only with parties we need to run Gentle, such as Google Firebase for storage and sign-in, Stripe, Apple and Google for payments and an email service for service emails. We have agreements with them about protecting your data.

How long

For as long as you have an account. If you delete your account, we delete your data, except what we're legally required to keep, such as payment records for accounting.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data and object to how it's used. You can delete your account yourself, see Delete your account. For other requests, email us. If you disagree with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or your local authority.
